About This Forecast

This prediction uses an XGBoost machine learning model trained on 11 years of historical weather and bloom data from Bonn (2015-2025). The model analyzes over 60 weather features to predict cherry blossom timing.


Weather Data Source: Daily meteorological data is obtained from the Meteostat API, which aggregates weather observations from Station 10517 (Bonn Friesdorf). The data is updated daily with the latest measurements.


Bloom Data Source: Historical cherry blossom bloom dates were manually sampled from kirschbluete-bonn.de, the official source for Bonn's cherry blossom information, providing accurate ground-truth data for model training.

📊 Model Features ▼
  • Temperature Features: Daily min/max/average temperatures, monthly aggregates
  • Chilling Requirements: Accumulated hours below 7.2°C (winter dormancy)
  • Growing Degree Days: Heat accumulation above 10°C baseline
  • Seasonal Patterns: Winter, spring, summer, and fall averages
  • Precipitation: Monthly rainfall and snow accumulation
  • Rolling Averages: 7, 14, 30, 60, and 90-day temperature trends
  • Photoperiod: Day length calculations based on latitude
  • Previous Year Influence: Fall and winter conditions from previous year
🌡️ Data Collection ▼
  • Station: Bonn Friesdorf (ID: 10517)
  • Location: 50.7°N, 7.1°E
  • Update Frequency: Daily at 06:00 UTC
  • Parameters: Temperature, precipitation, wind, pressure, snow
  • Historical Range: 2015-2025 training data
  • Quality Control: Automated validation and gap-filling by Meteostat
🌸 Historical Bloom Data ▼
  • Data Source: kirschbluete-bonn.de
  • Collection Method: Manual sampling of official bloom reports
  • Years Covered: 2015-2025 (11 years of observations)
  • Data Points: Bloom start dates, peak bloom, and end dates
  • Location: Heerstraße and Breite Straße in Bonn Altstadt
  • Validation: Cross-referenced with local observations and photos
