Healthy Subjects

Low depression scores (2.5-4.6) with consistently high MPAS values (84.2-89.6). Shows minimal fluctuation over time with a slight positive trend in attachment.

Anxiety Disorder

Moderate depression scores that peak at T1 (10.4) and then improve (to 5.2). MPAS values show steady improvement from 76.8 to 82.7, suggesting recovery in maternal-infant bonding.

Postpartum Depression

High depression scores that worsen over time (8.4 to 13.0). MPAS values decline steadily (69.5 to 60.2), showing a clear negative correlation between depression severity and maternal attachment.

Key Insights

  • The postpartum depression group shows the most dramatic anti-correlation, with increasing depression and decreasing attachment.
  • The anxiety disorder group shows improvement in both metrics over time, suggesting effective treatment.
  • The healthy group maintains stable, optimal levels in both measures, serving as a control baseline.
  • The visualization reveals how the relationship between depression and attachment varies significantly based on the underlying condition.
